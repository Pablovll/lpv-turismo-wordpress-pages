Manifesto

Travel means entering into a relationship with the city.

LPV Turismo was created to reveal a Rio that is more alive, deeper and less automatic. Our tours combine landscape, culture, history, urban life, nature and conversation, so every visitor feels cared for, oriented and invited to look at the city with more attention.

It is not only about passing by tourist highlights. It is about creating an experience with meaning.

About me

An Argentine guide sharing Rio from the inside.

LPV Turismo is led by Luis Pablo Vallasciani, a tour guide with a warm, cultural and territory-sensitive approach. The idea is to welcome visitors with care, clarity and good stories, creating bridges between those who arrive and a city that lives beyond the postcards.

  • Service in Portuguese, Spanish and English.
  • Tours for small groups, families and private experiences.
  • Focus on culture, nature, city life, photography, gastronomy and everyday Rio.
  • Experiences adapted to the profile, energy and interests of the group.
