Experience overview

A tour for reading Rio beyond the postcards.

Mosaics of Rio is an urban experience for travelers who want to observe the city with more attention: its historical layers, rhythms, streets, empty spaces, encounters and contrasts. The route can be adapted to the group profile, available time and personal interests.

Profile

Ideal for travelers interested in culture, photography, architecture, urban art, everyday life and city reading.

Format

Private or small-group experience, with flexible pace and adaptation according to participants' interests.

Languages

Service available in Portuguese, Spanish and English, with a clear, warm and group-sensitive approach.

Experience atmosphere

Rio in layers, details and urban scenes.

A visual selection that translates the spirit of Mosaics of Rio: streets, art, urban landscape and cultural details that help tell the city.

Concept

The city as a composition of fragments.

Rio is not a single image. It is a set of scenes, neighborhoods, sounds, stories and ways of occupying space. This route starts from that idea: looking at the city as a mosaic, where each detail helps understand a larger part of the Carioca landscape.

01

Urban landscape

Observation of streets, squares, facades, perspectives, contrasts between nature and concrete and ways of moving through the city.

02

Everyday culture

Conversations about habits, rhythms, encounters, local commerce, occupation of public space and forms of Carioca sociability.

03

Art and memory

Reading of artistic expressions, architecture, urban symbols and historical marks that appear along the way.

What we will explore

An experience designed to observe, talk and connect.

The content of the route may vary according to the starting point, the conditions of the day and the group profile. The proposal is always to build a living experience, not a rigid itinerary.

Neighborhoods and paths

Reading of urban spaces, their transitions, atmospheres and relationships between different areas of the city.

Architecture and city

Observation of styles, facades, adaptations, marks of time and contrasts between formal projects and real life.

Photography and gaze

Pauses to contemplate, photograph and notice scenes that would normally go unnoticed.

Stories and context

Accessible explanations about city, culture, landscape, memory and everyday life, without turning the tour into a heavy class.

Local rhythm

Time to walk, talk, stop, observe, have a coffee or adjust the route according to the group's energy.

Tailor-made experience

Possibility of adapting the route to interests such as art, gastronomy, photography, music, urban history or neighborhood life.

Format and logistics

How the tour works.

Before confirming, we align expectations, language, pace, interests and specific needs.

01

Initial conversation

02

Route curation

03

Guided experience

On the day of the tour, the focus is context, care, safety, good stories and freedom to adjust the pace.

Includes

  • Private guiding or small-group guiding.
  • Route curation according to the visitor's profile.
  • Cultural, urban and historical context during the experience.
  • Service in Portuguese, Spanish or English.

Does not include

  • Transportation, when necessary. Ask about transfer options.
  • Food, drinks and personal expenses.
  • Tickets or entrance fees, if the route includes paid spaces.
  • Third-party services not previously arranged.

Recommendations

  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Bring water, sunscreen and ID.
  • Bring a phone or camera for photos.
  • Inform mobility restrictions or preferences before the tour.
