Experience overview

An encounter with the forest inside the city.

Tijuca Forest Immersion is an experience for travelers who want to live Rio de Janeiro beyond the beach and classic postcards. The route offers a sensitive approach to the Carioca Atlantic Forest, its paths, viewpoints, sounds, shade, stories and presence in the urban landscape.

Profile

Ideal for travelers interested in nature, photography, landscape, light ecotourism, environmental history and outdoor experiences.

Format

Private or small-group tour, adapted to the pace, interest and physical condition of participants.

Languages

Service in Portuguese, Spanish or English, with clear explanations and a close rhythm throughout the route.

Experience atmosphere

The forest in paths, viewpoints and silence.

A visual selection that translates the presence of the Atlantic Forest in Rio: vegetation, viewpoints and paths that reveal another dimension of the city.

Concept

The forest as an essential part of Rio.

The proposal of this route is to perceive that Rio is not just a city with nature around it. Nature is part of the city's own structure: it orients landscapes, marks neighborhoods, creates viewpoints, influences the climate, inspires ways of living and reveals a complex environmental history.

01

Living nature

Observation of the forest, its sounds, textures, shadows, paths and forms of presence in Carioca everyday life.

02

Landscape and viewpoints

Reading of the city from elevated points of view, connecting relief, neighborhoods, sea, mountains and forest.

03

Environmental history

Conversations about landscape transformation, reforestation, uses of the territory and the relationship between city and nature.

What we will explore

An experience of contemplation, movement and landscape interpretation.

The route may vary according to weather, conditions of the day, logistics and group profile. The idea is to build a safe, pleasant experience connected to the participants' rhythm.

Forest paths

Contact with trails, internal roads or circulation areas amid vegetation, always according to the agreed format.

Viewpoints and city reading

Pauses to observe Rio's geography and understand how mountains, sea, neighborhoods and forest relate.

Photography and contemplation

Time to record landscapes, forest details and moments of silence or observation.

Stories of the territory

Context about occupation, landscape transformation and the importance of Tijuca Forest to Carioca identity.

Adaptable rhythm

The experience can be more contemplative, photographic, cultural or active, according to the group's interest.

Connection with real Rio

A way to understand the city not only through the urban, but also through the permanent presence of nature in its everyday life.
