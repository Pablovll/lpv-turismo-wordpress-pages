Local support

Other services

In addition to guided tours, LPV Turismo offers local support to make your stay in Rio more fluid: arrival, transportation, reservations, football, cultural experiences and tailor-made programming.

Complementary services

More than suggesting places: organizing the experience.

LPV Turismo can act as local support for those who want to arrive, move around and enjoy Rio with greater peace of mind. The proposal is to align expectations, care for possible logistics and suggest paths consistent with the visitor's profile.

Reception and local orientation

Reception of visitors, initial orientation about the city, arrival support, good circulation practices, neighborhood suggestions, basic organization of the first days and tailor-made assistance when necessary.

Logistics, transfers and transportation

Support to organize transportation during routes, arrival or departure from the city. When necessary, LPV Turismo can suggest transfer options, partner drivers or solutions suited to the size and profile of the group.

Reservations and local curation

Recommendations and support with gastronomic experiences, cultural programming, music, samba, viewpoints, restaurants, bars, family activities and experiences that match the traveler's style.

Football matchday and Maracana

Curation to experience Carioca football with context: guidance on available matches, arrival and departure logistics, cultural reading of the experience, fan atmosphere and the possibility of including Maracana or its surroundings according to calendar and availability.

Private experiences

Design of experiences for families, couples, small groups, international visitors, incentive trips or people who want a more authorial, safe Rio adapted to their own pace.

Group support

Organization of proposals for small groups with specific needs: languages, schedules, accessibility, cultural profile, photography, nature, gastronomy, football or a combination of services.

L&P Experiences

International journeys for select groups, combining business, culture, lifestyle and personal accompaniment. A tailor-made proposal for those seeking exclusive, well-cared-for experiences with refined curation.
